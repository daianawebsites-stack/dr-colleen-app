App launch NOT BUILT

Approved but not built: Expo config cannot express the hand-off or the caption, so what ships is the static Deep Indigo screen with the centred mark. This stays as the record of the intended motion.

The splash is the same every cold start, because it is the native launch screen and the app cannot vary it. What the app controls is the hand-off, and the hand-off depends on where the app is opening. Log in has a place for the mark to land, so it opens on the circle mark and shrinks it into that slot, with the tagline set separately beneath. Home and the quiz have nowhere to land, so they open on the full lockup instead — tagline included, no separate legend — and it leaves first, on its own over the indigo, before the indigo dissolves and something on the screen beneath takes the identity over. On a real device the splash is the native launch screen, so it must be a static image: Deep Indigo with the logo centred, no animation of its own. The expand comes free from iOS and Android. What the app controls is the hand-off, the 1.6s step here: one shared element kept mounted across the screen change, animating position and scale, rather than two screens cross-fading. On the two hand-offs that end without a mark, the shared element still carries the move — it just exits instead of landing, and the screen beneath starts its own entrance a beat later.

CaseWhat happens
Profile still loadingThe splash holds rather than dissolving. The launch screen is the loading state, so there is never a spinner where a logo just was. If the profile has not arrived 1.2s after the hold ends, dissolve anyway onto the app gradient with the spinner in the mark's place, so it can never look stuck.
Profile failed to loadDissolve exactly as normal, then the error state on the gradient. A failure is not a reason to change the way the app opens.
Reopened within 15 minutesNo splash and no motion. The app was suspended, not closed: iOS restores its own snapshot and the user is exactly where they left off. Anything else would be a lie about what happened.
Reopened after 15 minutesStill no splash, because the app never died. The screen cross-fades to Home over 240ms. Quiet, and clearly not a launch.
Reopened after 15 minutes, mid-flowNothing at all. The quiz, the paywall, the special offer, a password reset and the chat keep their place however long the absence, so there is nothing to animate.

Not drawn: states that only swap copy on an existing layout (empty states, trial counters, cancel flows, payment form, voice recording). They follow the same rules as the screens above.
