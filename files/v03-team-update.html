Team update · Branch v03

What's new in Get YU, version 3.

The topics for our update meeting. Everything below is built and checked, and goes up with this upload.

The idea behind this version: less to take in at once. The app shows her one thing at a time, in one place, and the assistant knows her whole app.

  1. Home and the assistant become one conversation

    • Home and the AI Assistant are now a single screen: one ongoing conversation that holds her day.
    • Daily cards arrive one at a time: news from Dr. Colleen, check-ins, follow-ups, the tip of the day and a weekly insight.
    • A calmer first visit: a short two-step welcome, then the assistant introduces itself. Nothing else arrives that day until she writes her first message.
    • One card at a time: a card arrives when she opens the app or comes back to it, never while she is in the middle of something.

  2. Daily check-ins

    • Quick one-tap questions through the day: mood, sleep, water, meals, symptoms and supplements.
    • Her answers fill her Tracker for her, and a logged check-in opens that part of her Tracker.
    • A weekly weigh-in: once a week, on the morning of her weigh-in day (Friday, unless she asks the assistant for another day), a check-in asks whether she'd like to log her weight.
    • The day after a conversation, a follow-up asks how it went.

  3. A smarter assistant

    • It knows her whole app: what she logs, her notes, her grocery list and what she has saved.
    • It remembers what she tells it, in a list called "Things to remember" that she can see and change.
    • It can answer with charts and tables drawn from her own data, and point out patterns between her habits and how she feels, carefully.
    • Voice messages and photos: she can record a voice message and see its words, and take or choose a photo, preview it and send it. Photos are stored privately; the assistant can't look at a photo yet, which comes with the developers' image reading.
    • It helps her find her way: "Where do I start?" and a tour of the app, one section at a time. After the welcome, a simple "yes" or "go" starts Dr. Colleen's guided start.
    • She can move her weekly weigh-in to another day just by asking.
    • When a question needs Dr. Colleen, it offers a direct way to message her.

  4. The Tracker

    • Trends: a weekly view with a chart for everything she tracks. Weight can be seen over 1, 3 or 6 months, as a chart or as a table.
    • Meals logged by breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks, with calories and nutrients against her goals.
    • Water, sleep and period tracking improved, including a predicted next period.
    • The quiz only accepts a realistic height and weight, so her targets start right.
    • She can change only the last three days (today, yesterday and the day before); older days stay safe as her history. The period calendar is the exception.

  5. Saving and her personal sections

    • Saving a recommendation from the chat is clearer: one "Add" button, one place to confirm.
    • My Supplements can show products Dr. Colleen has chosen.
    • A clear, honest note: Get YU doesn't sell supplements. Recommendations are ingredients, not brands, and a brand appears only when she asks for one.
    • My Notes is now My Notebook: her notes, conversation summaries and grocery list, with quantities.

  6. The Health Report

    • A new PDF design she can share with her doctor.
    • It now shows her daily goals and her supplement plan with Dr. Colleen's products.

  7. Resources and news from Dr. Colleen

    • A "From Dr. Colleen" section in Resources.
    • Articles open and read inside the app.
    • Dr. Colleen can schedule news posts that appear for the right members at the right time.

  8. Phone notifications

    • The app asks first, in a card that says clearly these are phone notifications, then the phone asks for permission.
    • At most two a day, in the morning and early evening, never while she is chatting. If she hasn't opened the app for two days, one a day.
    • A soft sound when something new arrives.
    • Simple yes or no settings in My Profile.

  9. The trial

    • Trial members see how many credits they have left: 15 to start.
    • Cards and check-ins never use credits.

  10. Account and privacy

    • She can download all her data or delete her account herself.
    • She sets her country, so the app shows the right emergency number.

  11. Look and feel

    • A brand refresh across every screen: colours, type and her own purple chat bubble.
    • A small celebration when she completes her day.

  12. Content Dr. Colleen can manage

    • Tips, health goals, news, resources and products now live where Dr. Colleen can review and edit them herself.
For the developers

Notes for the developers

High level only. The detail, with every file and decision, is in the documentation index in the repo (docs/v03-documentation-index.md), which will be refreshed after the upload.

The database

  • 24 changes to bring to the live Supabase, in orderNew tables, new fields and a few content fixes were made in the local database during v03. The live database needs the same changes, applied one after another in their numbered order.
  • A few change existing member data, so each runs exactly once, after a backupSome changes move or fix data members already have. For example, sleep entries move back one day, to the night they belong to. Running one twice would shift it twice, so take a backup first and run each only once.
  • New tables need access rights and a refreshA new table stays invisible to the app until it is given access rights and the database's list of tables is refreshed. Without that, the screens that read it come back empty.
  • The change numbers clash with the main branchThe main branch numbered its own database changes 13 and 14, the same numbers v03 uses. When the two branches are joined, the v03 changes need new numbers after main's.
  • The test data must not be copiedThe local database holds demo accounts, a demo news post, placeholder products and test pictures. They are for testing only and must stay out of the live database.

The assistant

  • It reads the whole app for every answerBefore each answer it reads her profile, the last 14 days of her Tracker, today's card answers, her notes and "Things to remember", her grocery list and what she has saved. Answers are more personal, and each one sends more to the AI.
  • Her profile follows what she logs, and a logged weight updates itWhen she has logged recently, the Tracker overrides her quiz answers, so the assistant and the screens use her latest numbers. A weight she logs also updates her profile and Health Report.
  • A short running summary is kept after each answerAfter every answer, a small extra AI step updates a short summary of the conversation, so the assistant keeps the thread even beyond its recent messages. She doesn't see it.
  • Every number is calculated by the serverCharts, tables, comparisons and weekly insights take their numbers from her data on the server. The AI only chooses what to show and writes the words around it, so it can't invent a figure.
  • Its rules have a new versionThe assistant's rulebook gained new rules in v03 (for example about her notes, her logged data and the days she can change), so it has a new version number.
  • The assistant now uses the real key, as main doesDuring testing the assistant ran through a temporary connection; it has been removed, so the server needs its real AI key (with a spending limit) to answer. The assistant's checks all passed before the switch; run them once more on the real key.

How the app behaves now

  • Home and chat are one screenThe separate chat screen is gone. Home is the conversation, and every link that used to open the chat now opens it.
  • Daily cards are stored in the history but never sent to the AI or counted as answersCheck-ins, news, tips and follow-ups are saved with the conversation as their own kind of message, so they show up on any phone. They are never passed to the AI as her words and never use trial credits.
  • Only the last three days can be changed, enforced by the server using her own dateShe can change today, yesterday and the day before, from the Tracker, the check-ins or the assistant. The app sends her own date with each change, and the server refuses anything older. The period calendar is the exception.
  • Notification choices are saved on her profileHer yes or no for check-ins, news, follow-ups, sounds and "Things to remember" are now stored on her profile, so they follow her to a new phone.

Before going live

  • A new app store releaseThis version adds phone features that an over-the-air update can't deliver: notifications, voice recording, the camera and photo library, and vibration. These can only be tried for real on the TestFlight build, and a checklist for that test is being prepared.
  • Reminders are scheduled by the phoneWhen she leaves the app, the phone schedules the next reminders itself and clears them when she comes back. There is no server job and no push setup. A news post published while her app stays closed waits for her next visit.
  • Voice and photosVoice messages are turned into text by an OpenAI service, so the live server needs its key; only the words are kept. Photos go to a private storage space, one folder per member, created by one of the database changes; they are included when she downloads her data and removed when she deletes her account. Reading what is in a photo is not built yet; the approved plan for how the assistant answers is ready for the developers.
  • A real email serviceSending emails (the contact form, account deletion) is still a placeholder that only records the message. It needs a real email service.
  • Trial creditsThe member sees 15 credits, which equal 5 tokens on the server. The server still has to report the tokens used. Every reply the server saves counts, as today; which scripted replies could be free is to review after the upload.
  • The testing shortcuts are removedThe demo account screen and its helpers are gone: the app opens on the real Log in. The demo accounts and sample content stay only as a reference, and 6 placeholder supplement products must be replaced with Dr. Colleen's real list before members can ask for a brand.
  • The automatic checks passEvery test and the architecture checks the pull request runs now pass.
  • Their open decisionsThe documentation index lists the technical decisions still open, each with a recommended answer.
